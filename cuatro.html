Topic: Daily routines | Scenario: Telling a partner what you do in a day

Listen to the full conversation

Interactive Dialogue

A: Hi, can you tell me what do you do in the morning?
B: Hello, I wake up early and go to school. What about you?
A: I wake up at six and have breakfast.
B: Do you go to school by bus?
A: Yes, I go by bus.
B: And, what do you do in the afternoon?
A: I do my homework and play with my friends.
B: That sounds nice.
A: Yes, it is, and in the evening, I watch TV.
B: Me too, but I have to go to bed early.
A: I see. Well, I have to leave now. Tomorrow I will continue telling you about my daily routine.
B: Ok. See you tomorrow, take care.
