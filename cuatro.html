Topic: Professions | Scenario: Sharing life goals in class

Dialogue

Teacher: Hello, guys. Today I want you to think about your future and your life goals. Please share what you want to be in the future.
Student A: What do you want to be in the future?
Student B: I want to be a doctor. And you?
Student A: I want to be a teacher.
Student A: Why do you want to be a doctor?
Student B: Because I like helping people.
Student A: That’s great. Where do you want to work?
Student B: I want to work at a hospital.
Student B: And you, where do you want to work?
Student A: I want to work at a school.
Student B: I hope you reach your goal.
Student A: Thank you, I wish you the best too.
Teacher: Thank you guys for sharing, you did an excellent job. Let’s listen to the next students.
