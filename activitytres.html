Topic 2: Ordering at a restaurant | Scenario: Restaurant

Listen to the full conversation

Dialogue

Waitress: Hello! Welcome to Daniela’s restaurant
Customer: Hello.
Waitress: Here is the menu. Let me know when you are ready to order
Customer: Thank you.
Waitress: What would you like to order?
Customer: For appetizer I would like garlic bread and grilled octopus, please
Waitress: All right, what would you like for the main dish?
Customer: I will have mushroom soup and spicy pasta, and for dessert, a blueberry cheesecake, please.
Waitress: Anything to drink?
Customer: Yes, a glass of red wine, please.
Waitress: Okay. Let me repeat your order: garlic bread and grilled octopus, mushroom soup and spicy pasta, blueberry cheesecake, and a glass of red wine. Is that correct?
Customer: Yes, that is correct.
Waitress: Perfect. Here you go, enjoy your meal!
Customer: Thank you!
