Role Play Activities

Topic: Part of the human body | Scenario: Visiting the doctor.

Listen to the full conversation

Dialogue

A: Good morning. How are you today?
B: Good morning. I don’t feel very well.
A: What’s the problem?
B: I have a headache and my throat hurts.
A: Do you have a fever?
B: Yes, a little.
A: Do you have pain in your chest?
B: No, my chest feels fine.
A: Does your stomach hurt?
B: Yes, a little, and my back hurts too.
A: Okay, it doesn’t seem to be anything serious. You should rest and drink plenty of water. You will be fine soon.
B: Doctor, what happens if I don’t feel better?
A: If you don’t improve or if your fever gets worse, you should come back.
B: Okay, thank you, doctor.
A: You’re welcome. Take care.
B: Goodbye.
