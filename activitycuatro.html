Role Play Activities

Topic 2: Giving directions | Scenario: Walking in the city.

Listen to the full conversation

Dialogue

Tourist: Hi, excuse me, I am lost.
Local: Oh, how can I help you?
Tourist: Where is Malecón 2000?
Local: It is near the Civil Register.
Tourist: How do I get there?
Local: Go straight for five blocks and then turn left at the bank.
Tourist: Ohh I see, would you mind writing it down on this paper, please?
Local: Sure. When you get to the bank, you will find a church, and in front of it is the Civil Register.
Tourist: It is too far.
Local: No, it is not. You are very close, keep walking straight along the same street and you will arrive at Malecon 2000, La Rotonda.
Tourist: Thank you very much.
Local: You’re welcome.
